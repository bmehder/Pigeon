# A brief history of resilient networks

{{ featured-image }}

Modern operators talk about redundancy, edge networks, and graceful degradation. Pigeon dispatchers were practicing all three before anyone had invented a status page.

## Every bird is an independent route

A flock does not rely on one vehicle, one road, or one increasingly nervous driver. Each courier carries a small payload and makes routing decisions close to the conditions that matter.

That architecture offers several enduring lessons:

- Small independent deliveries contain failures.
- Familiar destinations reduce coordination overhead.
- A network remains useful even when individual couriers stop for lunch.

The terminology has changed. The appeal of a system that continues working when one participant becomes fascinated by a fountain has not.
