# Pigeons and prevailing winds

Route planning gets complicated when half your workforce can feel a pressure change in their feathers and the other half is distracted by a newly opened bakery.

## Start with the wind, not the map

A direct line is not always the fastest flight. A modest detour behind a row of buildings can save energy, protect the payload, and reduce the likelihood of an unscheduled chimney inspection.

Good dispatchers review three things before assigning a route:

- Wind direction at rooftop height
- Safe places to pause without attracting gulls
- Whether the destination has recently installed reflective windows

The final decision still belongs to the pigeon. PigeonOps simply ensures that the pigeon begins with better information.
