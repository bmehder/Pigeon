# Dispatch without the rooftop shouting.

PigeonOps brings routes, couriers, weather, and snack expenditure into one exceptionally calm view. It is built for dispatchers who have better things to do than decode a sequence of increasingly emotional coos.

<aside class='content-note'>
  <strong>Designed around the flock.</strong>
  Every screen works with the instincts pigeons already have. No tiny keyboards required.
</aside>

## Routes that understand the sky

Most route planners think in roads. PigeonOps considers thermals, prevailing winds, protected landmarks, known hawk activity, and the opening hours of popular bakeries.

- Live rerouting when conditions change
- Rooftop-safe arrival zones
- Automatic avoidance of weddings and outdoor food markets
- Sensible allowances for staring at statues

## Every courier accounted for

See the location, assignment, and snack status of every bird in your operation. Dispatchers get a single source of truth; pigeons retain plausible deniability.

1. Assign a delivery to an available courier.
2. Review the recommended flight plan.
3. Follow progress from takeoff to windowsill.
4. Receive proof of delivery—or a candid explanation.

## Intelligence you can act on

PigeonOps turns millions of tiny journeys into decisions your team can use: which routes perform best, where delays begin, and whether premium sourdough crumbs improve morale enough to justify the expense.
