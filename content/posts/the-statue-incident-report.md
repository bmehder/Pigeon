# The statue incident report

Statues appear dependable. They do not move, complain, or reschedule deliveries. Unfortunately, this makes them extremely attractive places for a pigeon to hold an unscheduled meeting.

## What the data tells us

Most statue-related delays begin with one harmless landing and escalate when several colleagues notice. Within minutes, a routine route has become an all-hands architectural review.

The strongest preventative measures are surprisingly ordinary:

1. Route high-priority deliveries away from popular monuments.
2. Add five minutes when avoidance is impossible.
3. Never schedule a hungry flock through a public square at lunchtime.
4. Treat the phrase “I’ll only stop briefly” as an operational warning.

PigeonOps cannot eliminate professional curiosity. It can make room for it in the schedule.
