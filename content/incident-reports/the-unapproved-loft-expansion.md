# The unapproved loft expansion

{{ featured-image }}

At 08:40 on Monday, operations approved a modest shelf for spare message capsules. By 11:15, twelve pigeons had interpreted the shelf as premium residential accommodation.

## Sequence of events

The shelf was installed without a landing policy, reservation system, or explicit statement that it was not a penthouse. Early arrivals began defending the space while delivery traffic accumulated outside.

The review identified three corrective actions:

1. Classify every new horizontal surface before installation.
2. Publish occupancy rules before the flock discovers it.
3. Never describe storage as “spacious” within hearing distance of a pigeon.

Normal loft operations resumed after facilities added dividers and a clearly labelled capsule drawer.
